隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 9 月 23 日

本政策说明 KeepUp 当前如何处理数据。首次启动时的“同意并继续”只表示您阅读并同意本政策及用户协议，不代替定位、运动与健身、相机、照片、通知或广告的单独授权。

1. 运营方与联系方法

KeepUp 由ShiYue Bu提供。有关本政策或个人信息的疑问、查阅、更正、删除及撤回同意请求，请联系156788742@qq.com。我们会核实请求并在适用法律规定的期限内答复。

2. 我们处理的信息与用途

  • 您输入的资料：昵称、头像、性别、出生年份、身高、体重，以及您创建的打卡、备注、照片、卡片、目标和提醒，用于在 App 内展示、统计及编辑您的记录。
  • 运动数据：您启用计步或室内运动功能后，App 经 iOS 的运动与健身权限读取步数、距离等数据，用于每日目标及运动记录。
  • 位置数据：您主动开始户外跑步或骑行并授予定位权限后，App 在该次活动期间读取路线点、时间、速度等数据，用于轨迹、距离及运动统计；活动在后台继续时，定位可能继续运行。结束活动后停止定位。您可以在 iOS 设置中关闭权限。
  • 设备功能：您选择拍照、从相册选图、保存图片或设置提醒时，App 分别调用相机、照片及本地通知功能。拒绝可选权限不会阻止使用其他功能。
  • 购买信息：您查看、购买或恢复会员时，App 通过 Apple StoreKit 获取商品及经 Apple 验证的交易与权益状态，以展示价格和开通、恢复会员。支付资料由 Apple 处理，KeepUp 不读取银行卡号。
  • 网络请求：App 可从我们的 OSS 地址下载功能和商品展示配置；浏览本协议网页也会请求该地址。网络服务在传输时可能收到 IP 地址、请求时间等连接信息。该配置请求不包含您在 App 内保存的运动路线、头像或打卡内容。

3. 广告与第三方

KeepUp 集成 Google Mobile Ads 和 User Messaging Platform。当前远程配置中的广告开关为关闭状态。若以后启用广告，App 会在适用地区先展示独立的广告隐私选择，并仅在 SDK 返回可请求广告且其他应用条件满足时请求广告。初始协议同意不等于广告同意。启用广告时，Google 可能依据其服务处理设备标识、网络信息、广告互动及同意状态；具体内容受地区、设备权限和您的选择影响。您可通过 App 内出现的“广告隐私选项”重新管理选择。请参阅 Google 隐私政策。

购买及应用分发由 Apple 提供，相关处理可参阅 Apple 隐私政策。除上述服务及您主动分享、导出的情形外，当前版本不会把您保存的个人资料、打卡内容或运动路线上传到 KeepUp 自有服务器。当前版本未实现 iCloud 同步；页面中的 iCloud 展示不代表该功能已经可用。

4. 存储、保留与删除

您的个人资料及活动记录主要保存在设备上的应用数据中；您删除单条记录时，App 删除对应的本地内容。卸载 App 通常会移除该设备上的应用数据，但系统备份、您自行导出的文件及 Apple、Google 各自持有的数据受其相应设置与政策管理。您可在 iOS 设置中撤回系统权限，在 App 内管理广告选择，或通过上述联系方法提出与我们处理的信息有关的请求。当前版本没有 KeepUp 账号，因此不存在 App 内账号注销流程。

5. 未成年人

未成年人使用 KeepUp 时应由监护人阅读本政策并指导其使用。对于不满 14 周岁的用户，本节为专门规则：其父母或其他监护人应在使用前阅读并同意我们按第 2 节所列范围、用途和第 4 节所列保留方式处理信息；如不同意，请不要让该用户填写个人资料或使用相关功能。监护人可通过第 1 节的联系方式请求查阅、更正或删除相关信息。若您认为我们不应处理某位未成年人的信息，请联系我们核实并处理。

6. 政策更新

我们会在本页面公布更新后的政策及日期。若处理目的、方式或信息种类发生重大变化，我们会在适用法律要求的情况下另行告知并取得所需同意。

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy describes how the current version of KeepUp handles data. “Agree and Continue” means you have read and accepted this policy and the User Agreement. It does not replace separate system permissions or any advertising consent choice.

1. Provider and contact

KeepUp is provided by ShiYue Bu. For questions or requests to access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for personal information, contact 156788742@qq.com. We will verify and respond to requests within the period required by applicable law.

2. Information and purposes

  • Information you enter: Your nickname, profile image, gender, birth year, height, weight, check-ins, notes, photos, cards, goals, and reminders are used to display, calculate, and edit your records in the app.
  • Motion data: When you enable step counting or indoor activities, the app reads steps and distance through the iOS Motion & Fitness permission for daily goals and activity records.
  • Location data: When you start an outdoor run or ride and grant permission, the app reads route points, time, and speed for maps, distance, and activity statistics. Location may continue while that activity runs in the background and stops when the activity ends. You can disable access in iOS Settings.
  • Device features: Camera, Photos, and local notifications are used when you choose to take or select a photo, save an image, or set a reminder. Declining an optional permission does not prevent use of other features.
  • Purchases: When you view, buy, or restore membership, the app uses Apple StoreKit product and verified transaction or entitlement status to show prices and provide access. Apple handles payment details; KeepUp does not read your bank card number.
  • Network requests: The app may download feature and product display configuration from our OSS address. Viewing this policy also makes a request to that address. The network service may receive an IP address, request time, and similar connection information. The configuration request does not include your saved route, profile image, or check-in content.

3. Advertising and third parties

KeepUp includes Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform. Advertising is disabled in the current remote configuration. If enabled later, the app will present a separate advertising privacy choice where required and request ads only when the SDK permits it and the app's other conditions are met. Agreeing to this policy is not advertising consent. When ads are enabled, Google may process device identifiers, network information, ad interactions, and consent status according to your region, device permissions, and choices. Where shown, you can revisit “Ad privacy options” in the app. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Apple provides purchases and app distribution; see Apple's Privacy Policy. Apart from these services and content you choose to share or export, the current app does not upload your saved profile, check-ins, or exercise routes to a KeepUp-operated server. iCloud sync is not implemented in the current version; an iCloud illustration in the app does not mean that feature is available.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

Your profile and activity records are primarily stored in the app's data on your device. Deleting an individual record removes its associated local content. Uninstalling the app normally removes its data from that device. System backups, files you export, and data held by Apple or Google are governed by their respective settings and policies. You can revoke system permissions in iOS Settings, manage advertising choices in the app when available, or contact us about information we process. The current version has no KeepUp account and therefore no in-app account deletion flow.

5. Children

A parent or guardian should read this policy and guide a minor's use of KeepUp. For a user under 14, this section provides specific rules: a parent or other guardian should read and agree before use to the categories and purposes in section 2 and retention practices in section 4. If the guardian does not agree, the minor should not enter personal information or use the related features. A guardian may use the contact in section 1 to request access, correction, or deletion. If you believe we should not process information about a minor, contact us so we can verify and address the request.

6. Changes

We will post an updated policy and date on this page. If purposes, methods, or categories of processing change materially, we will give additional notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law.
